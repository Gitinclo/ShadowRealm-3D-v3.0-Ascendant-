# Artifact Forging

> **Project:** ShadowRealm 3D — v3.0 Ascendant | **Category:** Cultivation, Worldbuilding & Game Systems | **Last Updated:** 2026-11-25

Rarity scaling and stat generation for NFT gear.

## Scaling Formula

Rarity tiers `0..5` (Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary, Mythic) scale multiplicatively by `2^rarity`: `STR = 4 * 2^r`, `DEF = 2 * 2^r`, `CC Value = 18 * 2^r`.
