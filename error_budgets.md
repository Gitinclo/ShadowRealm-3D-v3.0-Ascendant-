# Error Budgets

> **Project:** ShadowRealm 3D — v3.0 Ascendant | **Category:** SRE, DevOps & Operational Discipline | **Last Updated:** 2026-11-25

Policy linking reliability burn rate to feature velocity.

## Budget Policy

When monthly Web availability or CI green-rate error budget exceeds 50% burn, freeze non-critical feature merges and prioritize reliability, regression fixes, and `TECH_DEBT.md`.
