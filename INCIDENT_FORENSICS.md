# Incident Forensics

> **Project:** ShadowRealm 3D — v3.0 Ascendant | **Category:** Security, Threat Modeling & Cryptography | **Last Updated:** 2026-11-25

Evidence preservation steps during security incidents.

## Procedure

Snapshot failing workflow logs, commit hashes, SHA-256 checksums of affected artifacts, and preserve timeline before rotating compromised credentials.
