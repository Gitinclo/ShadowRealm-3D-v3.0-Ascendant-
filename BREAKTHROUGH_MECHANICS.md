# Breakthrough Mechanics

> **Project:** ShadowRealm 3D — v3.0 Ascendant | **Category:** Cultivation, Worldbuilding & Game Systems | **Last Updated:** 2026-11-25

Leveling, XP curve, and bottleneck breakthroughs.

## XP Scaling

Each level requires `floor(100 * 1.22^(level - 1))` XP (`main_desktop.cpp` / `src/game/rules.ts`). Breakthroughs grant `+18 MaxHP`, full HP/Stamina restoration, `+3 STR`, `+2 DEF`, `+1 AGI`, and `+2 Domain Levels`.
