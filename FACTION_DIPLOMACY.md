# Faction Diplomacy

> **Project:** ShadowRealm 3D — v3.0 Ascendant | **Category:** Cultivation, Worldbuilding & Game Systems | **Last Updated:** 2026-11-25

Inter-order alliances and rivalries across the four biomes.

## Dynamics

The Iron Citadel (Knights), Astral Spire (Mages), Nightblade Guild (Rogues), and Cinder Circle (Shamans) maintain a fragile truce against the Shadow King's legions.
