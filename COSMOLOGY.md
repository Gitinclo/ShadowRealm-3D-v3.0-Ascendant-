# Cosmology

> **Project:** ShadowRealm 3D — v3.0 Ascendant | **Category:** Cultivation, Worldbuilding & Game Systems | **Last Updated:** 2026-11-25

Metaphysical structure of the ShadowRealm multiverse and cultivation planes.

## Planes of Existence

1. **Mortal ShadowRealm:** Four concentric zones radiating outward from the Shadow Spire.
2. **Astral Abyss:** High-density Yin Qi veil fueling the Infinite Devouring Domain.
3. **Ascendant Firmament:** Upper realm reached only when a cultivator transcends Domain Level 999 and survives the Final Heavenly Tribulation.
