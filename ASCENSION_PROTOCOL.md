# Ascension Protocol

> **Project:** ShadowRealm 3D — v3.0 Ascendant | **Category:** Cultivation, Worldbuilding & Game Systems | **Last Updated:** 2026-11-25

Endgame transcendence at Domain Level 999.

## Requirements

Attain Domain Level 999, conquer the Shadow King in the Cursed Wastes during Nightfall, and bind at least one Mythic relic to trigger Ascendant status.
