> **Project:** ShadowRealm 3D — v3.0 Ascendant | **Category:** Cultivation, Worldbuilding & Game Systems | **Last Updated:** 2026-11-25

Spatial layout of the four concentric zones around the Shadow Spire.

## Radial Zones

| Zone | Radius from Origin | Visual Tint | Threat Profile |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Shadow Forest** | `0 – 32u` | Emerald-shadow mist | Shadow Wolves, starter camps, Shadow Spire landmark |
| **Ashen Desert** | `32 – 54u` | Scorched amber dust | Rift Goblins, ember storms |
| **Obsidian Mountains** | `54 – 76u` | Indigo basalt ridges | Void Shades, high-altitude ambushers |
| **Cursed Wastes** | `> 76u` | Violet void corruption | Elite packs & Shadow King boss spawns |
