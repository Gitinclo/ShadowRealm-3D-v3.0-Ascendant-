# Qi Economy

> **Project:** ShadowRealm 3D — v3.0 Ascendant | **Category:** Cultivation, Worldbuilding & Game Systems | **Last Updated:** 2026-11-25

Resource loops connecting Stamina, Gold, Chaos Coins (CC), and XP.

## Flows

- **Stamina (Qi Flow):** Regenerates passively (`16/s`), consumed by sprinting (`22/s`) and combat maneuvers.
- **Gold:** Earned from enemies (`14 + 3*lv`), wave clears (`+40G`), spent on potions and Shadow Essence.
- **Chaos Coins (CC):** Earned from kills, streaks (`on_fire` +25/+50 CC), and NFT dismantling; spent on XP Scrolls, 2× CC Multipliers, and Nuke Resets.
