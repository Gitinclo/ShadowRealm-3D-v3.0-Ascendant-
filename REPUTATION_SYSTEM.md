# Reputation System

> **Project:** ShadowRealm 3D — v3.0 Ascendant | **Category:** Cultivation, Worldbuilding & Game Systems | **Last Updated:** 2026-11-25

Standing rewards tied to wave clears and boss vanquishing.

## Milestones

Clearing full enemy waves grants bounty gold (`+40G`), while reaching 200 CC triggers an Epic Relic airdrop from the Ascendant Vault.
