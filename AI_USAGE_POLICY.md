# AI Usage Policy

> **Project:** ShadowRealm 3D — v3.0 Ascendant | **Category:** Legal, Compliance & Trust | **Last Updated:** 2026-11-25

Guidelines for AI-assisted code, art, and documentation contributions.

## Rules

AI-assisted PRs are welcome when the contributor reviews, understands, and tests every changed line. Never submit unverified hallucinations, broken paths, or unattributed copyrighted material.
