# Severity Definitions

> **Project:** ShadowRealm 3D — v3.0 Ascendant | **Category:** Quality Assurance & Verification | **Last Updated:** 2026-11-25

Classification rubric for bugs and incidents.

## Levels

- **SEV-1 (Critical):** Crash on boot, data corruption, or active security vulnerability.
- **SEV-2 (High):** Broken primary gameplay loop, CI failure on `main`, or severe FPS drop.
- **SEV-3 (Medium):** Non-blocking UI glitch, minor balance outlier, or secondary platform warning.
- **SEV-4 (Low):** Cosmetic polish, typo, or lore wording tweak.
