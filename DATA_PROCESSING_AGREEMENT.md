# Data Processing Agreement (DPA)

> **Project:** ShadowRealm 3D — v3.0 Ascendant | **Category:** Legal, Compliance & Trust | **Last Updated:** 2026-11-25

Baseline data-handling terms for any hosted deployment.

## Scope

Because ShadowRealm 3D operates offline-first without collecting personal data, no user PII is processed by the core engine. Hosting providers act as infrastructure processors under their standard DPAs.
