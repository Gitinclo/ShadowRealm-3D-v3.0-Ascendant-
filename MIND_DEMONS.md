# Mind Demons

> **Project:** ShadowRealm 3D — v3.0 Ascendant | **Category:** Cultivation, Worldbuilding & Game Systems | **Last Updated:** 2026-11-25

Risk-reward instability mechanics during low vitality and nightfall.

## Manifestation

During Nightfall (`dayTime` 66s–114s), ambient Yin surges accelerate enemy speed (`1.2×`) and widen aggro radius (`24u`), testing composure when HP drops below the 20% `low_hp_warn` threshold.
