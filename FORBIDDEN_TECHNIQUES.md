# Forbidden Techniques

> **Project:** ShadowRealm 3D — v3.0 Ascendant | **Category:** Cultivation, Lore & Economy | **Last Updated:** 20/11/2026

High-impact endgame arts governed by strict Causality unlocks.

## Arts

- **Teraton Bomb (`N`):** Unlocked via `unlock_nuke` (Lv >= 5, Kills >= 10), deals `450 + level * 40` crit Burn damage across a `30u` radius on a `90s` cooldown.
- **Void Erasure (Domain Lv 25) & Absolute Zero (Domain Lv 250):** Instant execution below 20% HP and realm-wide freeze.
