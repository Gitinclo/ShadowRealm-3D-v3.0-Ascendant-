# Deprecation Policy

> **Project:** ShadowRealm 3D — v3.0 Ascendant | **Category:** Core Repository & Governance | **Last Updated:** 2026-11-25

Lifecycle rules for retiring APIs, rule schemas, and build targets.

## Window & Notice

Deprecated interfaces remain supported for at least one minor release with compiler `[[deprecated]]` annotations or console warnings, and are logged in `RELEASE_NOTES.md` and `MIGRATION.md` before removal.
