# Tribulation System

> **Project:** ShadowRealm 3D — v3.0 Ascendant | **Category:** Cultivation, Worldbuilding & Game Systems | **Last Updated:** 2026-11-25

Escalating boss trials triggered by cultivation milestones.

## Heavenly & Boss Tribulations

Reaching Level 10 or accumulating 3 defeats triggers the `boss_wave` Causality rule, summoning the Shadow King with scaled vitality (`360 * (1 + 0.16 * (lv-1))`) and legendary loot tables.
