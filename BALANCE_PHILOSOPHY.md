# Balance Philosophy

> **Project:** ShadowRealm 3D — v3.0 Ascendant | **Category:** Cultivation, Worldbuilding & Game Systems | **Last Updated:** 2026-11-25

Principles governing power curves from Lv 1 to Domain Lv 999.

## Design Pillars

Early levels reward tactical kiting and potions; mid-game unlocks explosive AoE power spikes (Teraton Bomb, Domain Lv 25+); nightfall and scaled Shadow King encounters preserve lethal stakes without punishing experimentation.
