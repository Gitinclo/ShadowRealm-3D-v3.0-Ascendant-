> **Project:** ShadowRealm 3D — v3.0 Ascendant | **Category:** Cultivation, Worldbuilding & Game Systems | **Last Updated:** 2026-11-25

Spatial AoE geometry of the Infinite Devouring Domain.

## Domain Geometry

Radius scales linearly with Domain Level: `min(80, 12 + (domainLevel - 1) * 0.5)`. Cooldown compresses to a 15s floor: `max(15, 40 - domainLevel * 0.25)`.
