# Accessibility QA

> **Project:** ShadowRealm 3D — v3.0 Ascendant | **Category:** Quality Assurance & Verification | **Last Updated:** 2026-11-25

Visual contrast, keyboard navigation, and input ergonomics checks.

## Checks

- High-contrast HUD health/stamina bars and legible monospace typography.
- Full keyboard-only control support on Desktop/Web (`WASD`, `Z/X/C/G/N`, `Tab`, `Esc`).
- Clear audio/visual telegraphing for boss alerts and low-HP warnings.
