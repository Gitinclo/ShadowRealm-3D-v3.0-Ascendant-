# RFC Process

> **Project:** ShadowRealm 3D — v3.0 Ascendant | **Category:** Core & Governance | **Last Updated:** 20/11/2026

Lightweight proposal process for major engine or gameplay changes.

## Steps

1. Copy `docs/rfcs/TEMPLATE.md` into `docs/rfcs/NNNN-proposal.md`.
2. Open a PR for community and maintainer review.
3. On acceptance, record an ADR in `docs/adr/` and update `ROADMAP.md`.
