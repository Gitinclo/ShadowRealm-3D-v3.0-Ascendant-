# Localization QA

> **Project:** ShadowRealm 3D — v3.0 Ascendant | **Category:** Quality Assurance & Verification | **Last Updated:** 2026-11-25

Guidelines for UTF-8 safety and future string externalization.

## Standards

- All source, CMake, HTML, and Markdown files strictly UTF-8 without BOM.
- Avoid hardcoded text truncation that breaks longer translations in HUD overlays.
