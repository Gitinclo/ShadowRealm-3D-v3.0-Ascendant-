# Calamities & Extinctions

> **Project:** ShadowRealm 3D — v3.0 Ascendant | **Category:** Cultivation, Lore & Economy | **Last Updated:** 20/11/2026

World-scale crisis events shaping the history of ShadowRealm.

## Major Calamities

- **The First Eclipse:** Shattered the ancient dawn and created the 120-second Day/Night oscillation.
- **The Cursed Wastes Blight:** Corrupted the outer ring (`r > 76u`) into the Shadow King's dominion.
