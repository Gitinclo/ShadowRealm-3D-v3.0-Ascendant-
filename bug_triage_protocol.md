# Bug Triage Protocol

> **Project:** ShadowRealm 3D — v3.0 Ascendant | **Category:** Quality Assurance & Verification | **Last Updated:** 2026-11-25

Workflow for labeling, prioritizing, and assigning incoming issues.

## Process

1. Reproduce with platform, commit SHA, and logs.
2. Assign severity per `severity_definitions.md`.
3. Label subsystem (`desktop`, `android`, `web`, `lore`, `balance`) and link regression test on fix.
