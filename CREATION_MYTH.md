The sundered origin of Light, Shadow, and the First Spire.

## The Sundering of Dawn

Before eras were counted, the Primordial Dao split into Radiant Yang and Devouring Yin. Where the two currents collided, the obsidian Shadow Spire crystallized, anchoring four shifting biomes and seeding the first Spirit Roots across the wastes.
