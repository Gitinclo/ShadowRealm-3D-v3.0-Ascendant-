# Project Governance

> **Project:** ShadowRealm 3D — v3.0 Ascendant | **Category:** Core & Governance | **Last Updated:** 20/11/2026

Decision-making, stewardship, and RFC approval model.

## Model

ShadowRealm 3D uses a Maintainer-led consensus model documented in `MAINTAINERS.md` and `RBAC_MATRIX.md`. Major architectural or balance shifts go through `RFC_PROCESS.md` and are recorded in `DECISION_LOG.md` and `docs/adr/`.
