# Ethics Charter

> **Project:** ShadowRealm 3D — v3.0 Ascendant | **Category:** Legal, Compliance & Trust | **Last Updated:** 2026-11-25

Ethical design commitments for gameplay and community governance.

## Commitments

- **No Predatory Monetization:** In-game NFTs, Gold, and Chaos Coins are purely earned in-game simulation mechanics—no real-money gambling or dark-pattern loot boxes.
- **Inclusive Community:** Respectful, constructive technical and creative collaboration.
