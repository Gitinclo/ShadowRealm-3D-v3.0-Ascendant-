# Technique Synergies

> **Project:** ShadowRealm 3D — v3.0 Ascendant | **Category:** Cultivation, Worldbuilding & Game Systems | **Last Updated:** 2026-11-25

Optimal combat combinations across classes and statuses.

## Synergies

- **Frostfire + Domain:** Mage's Freeze slows enemies (`0.45×`), trapping them inside the 8s Devouring Domain tick zone.
- **Streak + 2× CC Multiplier:** Chain kills to 5+ streak to proc `on_fire` for `+50 CC`.
- **Stun Lock + Primary Crit:** Knight's `Aegis Cleave` halts enemy movement (`0×`) to safely land 4× crits.
