# Soul Imprinting

> **Project:** ShadowRealm 3D — v3.0 Ascendant | **Category:** Cultivation, Worldbuilding & Game Systems | **Last Updated:** 2026-11-25

Binding NFT relics and weapons to a cultivator's active loadout.

## Mechanics

Equipping a Relic/NFT imprints its `strBonus` and `defBonus` directly into `totalStr()` and `totalDef()`. Unbinding or dismantling converts the imprint into Chaos Coins (`18 * 2^rarity`).
