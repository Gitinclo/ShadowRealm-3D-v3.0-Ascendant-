# Demonic vs. Righteous Paths

> **Project:** ShadowRealm 3D — v3.0 Ascendant | **Category:** Cultivation, Lore & Economy | **Last Updated:** 20/11/2026

Philosophical and mechanical divergence between Devouring and Astral cultivation.

## Divergence

- **Righteous Path:** Emphasizes defensive mitigation, Revive Tokens, and steady sect reputation.
- **Devouring (Demonic) Path:** Accelerates **Infinite Devouring Domain** growth through relentless kill streaks (`on_fire`) and aggressive Nightfall hunting.
