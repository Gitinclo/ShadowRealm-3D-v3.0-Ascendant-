# Auction House Mechanics

> **Project:** ShadowRealm 3D — v3.0 Ascendant | **Category:** Cultivation, Lore & Economy | **Last Updated:** 20/11/2026

Design specification for player-driven relic trading and Chaos Coin pricing.

## Design

Future multiplayer shards support escrow-backed relic auctions priced in Gold and Chaos Coins (`CC`), with rarity-anchored price floors (`18 * 2^rarity` base CC value) and anti-snipe bid extensions.
