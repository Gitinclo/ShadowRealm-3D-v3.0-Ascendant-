# User Acceptance Testing (UAT) Checklist

> **Project:** ShadowRealm 3D — v3.0 Ascendant | **Category:** Quality Assurance & Verification | **Last Updated:** 2026-11-25

Release sign-off criteria from a player perspective.

## Checklist

- [ ] All 4 classes selectable with accurate stats and signature powers.
- [ ] Day/night lighting and 4 zone tints visibly transition.
- [ ] NFT equip/unequip/sell updates STR/DEF and CC immediately.
- [ ] Pause (`Esc`) and Grimoire (`Tab`) open and close cleanly.
