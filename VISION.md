# Vision

> **Project:** ShadowRealm 3D — v3.0 Ascendant | **Category:** Core Repository & Governance | **Last Updated:** 2026-11-25

Long-term architectural and creative direction for ShadowRealm 3D.

## North Star

Deliver a self-contained, high-craft dark-fantasy & cultivation action RPG that runs natively on Desktop (OpenGL 3.3 Core / C++17), Android NDK (OpenGL ES 3.2 & Vulkan 1.3 HDR10+), and modern WebGL browsers with deterministic combat and reactive world causality.

## Three-Year Horizon

- Expand the 4-zone radial open world into multi-realm ascensions.
- Achieve full cross-platform save and deterministic combat parity between C++17 (`main_desktop.cpp`, `Causality.h`) and Web (`src/game/`).
- Support community-authored Causality v2 rule packs and cultivation sects.
