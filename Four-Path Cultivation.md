# Four-Path Cultivation System

> **Project:** ShadowRealm 3D — v3.0 Ascendant | **Category:** Cultivation, Lore & Economy | **Last Updated:** 20/11/2026

Canonical cultivation framework linking the four ShadowRealm archetypes to Dao progression.

## The Four Paths

| Path | Archetype | Primary Meridian | Signature Dao Technique | Status Affinity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Path of the Iron Bastion** | Shadow Knight | Iron Vein (STR/DEF) | `Aegis Cleave` | Stun (`STATUS_STUN`) |
| **Path of the Astral Frostfire** | Void Mage | Dual Frost/Flame Root | `Frostfire Nova` | Freeze + Burn |
| **Path of the Phantom Gale** | Nightblade Rogue | Gale Meridian (AGI) | `Venom Flurry` | Poison (`STATUS_POISON`) |
| **Path of the Eclipse Soul** | Eclipse Shaman | Cinder/Soul Vessel | `Cinder Totem` | Burn (`STATUS_BURN`) |

## Unified Ascension Curve

All four paths converge through the **Infinite Devouring Domain** (Lv 1 → Lv 999), unlocking Soul Devour (Lv 1), Domain Suppression (Lv 10), Void Erasure (Lv 25), Breaker (Lv 50), Chaos (Lv 100), Absolute Zero (Lv 250), and Absolute Supremacy (Lv 999).
