# Talisman Inscription

> **Project:** ShadowRealm 3D — v3.0 Ascendant | **Category:** Cultivation, Worldbuilding & Game Systems | **Last Updated:** 2026-11-25

Scrolls and cooldown resets used in tactical combat.

## Talismans

- **XP Scroll (50 CC):** Instant `+200 XP`.
- **CC Multiplier 2× (100 CC):** Doubles CC drops for 60s.
- **Nuke Cooldown Reset (200 CC):** Unlocks and resets Teraton Bomb immediately.
