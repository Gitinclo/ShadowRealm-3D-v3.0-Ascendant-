# Language & Runes

> **Project:** ShadowRealm 3D — v3.0 Ascendant | **Category:** Cultivation, Lore & Economy | **Last Updated:** 20/11/2026

In-world runic scripts inscribed onto relics, formations, and talismans.

## Runic Schools

Four calligraphic rune families correspond to the four Spirit Roots—Iron Sigils (Stun), Frostfire Glyphs (Freeze/Burn), Gale Marks (Poison/Crit), and Cinder Seals (Soul Drain).
